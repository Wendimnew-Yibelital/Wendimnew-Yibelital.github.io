Thesis & Field Work

Impact of the Land Registration and Certification Program (LRCP) on Women's Land Access and Control

ThesisChak Kebele, Hulet Eju Enessie Woreda

Examined how second-level land certification (SLLC) has affected women's tenure security and decision-making power over land, incorporating Proclamation No. 1324/2024. Analyzed survey data from 50 respondents using SPSS, finding high SLLC coverage, perceived improvements in tenure security, and statistically significant results linking land certification to household decision-making and marital status outcomes. Successfully defended in May 2026.

45-day externshipHulet Eju Enessie Woreda
